ICT SECURITY POLICY
Corporate Banking Group


1. Introduction

Information and Communication Technology (ICT) systems represent a critical asset for
Corporate Banking Group. This policy defines the principles and high-level rules governing
the protection of information systems, digital assets, and data processed by the organization.

The policy applies to all ICT environments, including on-premise infrastructure, cloud
services, and third-party managed platforms.


2. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to:
- Ensure confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets
- Reduce exposure to cyber risks
- Align ICT security practices with regulatory and supervisory expectations
- Establish a common security baseline across the organization


3. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and third parties
- All information systems and applications
- All data classified as internal, confidential, or restricted


4. Information Security Principles

4.1 Confidentiality  
Access to information must be granted strictly on a need-to-know basis.

4.2 Integrity  
Information must be protected against unauthorized modification or destruction.

4.3 Availability  
Systems and data must be available according to business continuity requirements.


5. Roles and Responsibilities

5.1 Board of Directors  
Provides strategic oversight on ICT and cybersecurity risks.

5.2 ICT Security Function  
Defines security standards, monitors compliance, and manages incidents.

5.3 System Owners  
Ensure security controls are implemented and maintained for assigned systems.


6. Policy Compliance

Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary actions and must be reported
to the ICT Security Function.


7. Review and Maintenance

This policy is reviewed annually or upon significant changes in the threat landscape
or regulatory requirements.
