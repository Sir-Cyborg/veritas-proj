ICT RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK
Corporate Banking Group


1. Introduction

ICT risks are a subset of operational risks and may arise from inadequate systems,
process failures, cyber threats, or external events.

This framework defines how ICT risks are identified, assessed, mitigated, and monitored.


2. Objectives

The objectives of the ICT Risk Management Framework are to:
- Ensure a consistent risk assessment methodology
- Support informed decision-making
- Maintain ICT risks within the approved risk appetite


3. Risk Identification

ICT risks are identified through:
- Periodic risk assessments
- Incident and problem management
- Audit findings
- Threat intelligence reports


4. Risk Assessment

4.1 Inherent Risk  
Assessment of risk before the application of controls.

4.2 Residual Risk  
Assessment of remaining risk after controls are applied.

Risk impact and likelihood are evaluated using a qualitative scoring model.


5. Risk Treatment

Identified risks may be:
- Mitigated through controls
- Accepted by management
- Transferred to third parties
- Avoided by discontinuing activities


6. Risk Monitoring and Reporting

Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) are defined and monitored on a quarterly basis.
Material ICT risks are reported to senior management and the Risk Committee.


7. Framework Review

This framework is reviewed annually and aligned with the enterprise-wide risk
management framework.
