CYBER INCIDENT MANAGEMENT POLICY
Corporate Banking Group


1. Introduction

Cyber incidents may impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability
of information systems and data.

This policy defines the approach to manage and respond to cyber incidents.


2. Definition of Cyber Incident

A cyber incident is any event that:
- Compromises information security
- Violates security policies
- Threatens ICT operations


3. Incident Classification

Incidents are classified based on:
- Impact
- Urgency
- Regulatory relevance


4. Incident Response Phases

4.1 Detection and Reporting  
All suspected incidents must be reported immediately.

4.2 Analysis and Containment  
The scope and root cause of the incident are assessed.

4.3 Eradication and Recovery  
Affected systems are restored securely.

4.4 Post-Incident Review  
Lessons learned are documented and corrective actions defined.


5. Roles and Responsibilities

The Cyber Incident Response Team coordinates response activities.


6. Regulatory Notification

Significant incidents must be reported to competent authorities
within defined timelines.


7. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually.
