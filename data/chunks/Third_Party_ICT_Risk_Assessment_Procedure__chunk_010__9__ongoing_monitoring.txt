9. Ongoing Monitoring

Ongoing monitoring must include:

- Annual (or more frequent for critical providers) reassessment of security posture and assurance reports.

- Review of SLA performance and incidents.

- Monitoring of significant changes (subprocessors, platform changes, data location changes).

- Concentration risk monitoring for critical vendors used across multiple services.

Any material adverse findings must be escalated to ICT Risk Management and relevant
governance committees.
