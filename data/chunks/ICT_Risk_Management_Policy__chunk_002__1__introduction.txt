1. Introduction

ICT risk is the risk of financial loss, service disruption, regulatory breach, or
reputational damage resulting from inadequate or failed internal processes, people, systems,
or from external events affecting ICT. In a banking context, ICT risk management must
support operational resilience, information security, and sound outsourcing practices.

This policy defines the governance model, minimum processes, and reporting requirements for
identifying, assessing, treating, monitoring, and reporting ICT risk within Corporate
Banking Group (CBG).
