4. Roles and Responsibilities

- Service Owner: defines business requirements, including RTO/RPO; ensures DR plans exist and tests are executed.

- Infrastructure Resilience Team: operates backup platforms, validates backups, and coordinates DR exercises.

- Application Teams: ensure application-level backup consistency, participate in recovery tests, and validate business functionality after recovery.

- Information Security: defines ransomware-resilient backup requirements and monitors control effectiveness.

- ICT Risk Management: monitors resilience risks and tracks remediation actions for failed tests or gaps.
