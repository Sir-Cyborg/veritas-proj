9. Emergency Changes

Emergency changes may be initiated for:

- P1 incidents requiring immediate restoration.

- Critical vulnerabilities requiring urgent mitigation.

Emergency change requirements:

- Document rationale and risk assessment (brief but explicit).

- Obtain expedited approval from authorized approver(s) (on-call manager/service owner).

- Execute with enhanced monitoring and maintain a clear rollback plan.

- Conduct a post-implementation review within 5 business days to validate controls and prevent recurrence.
