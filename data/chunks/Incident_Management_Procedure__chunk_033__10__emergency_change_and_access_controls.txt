10. Emergency Change and Access Controls During Incidents
During incidents, speed must not bypass control requirements:
- All production changes must be recorded and traceable.
- Emergency changes require:
  1) Documented rationale and risk assessment (brief but explicit)
  2) Approval by authorized approver (on-call manager/service owner)
  3) Backout plan
  4) Post-change validation steps

Privileged access must follow:
- Least privilege and time-bound access where possible
- Logging of privileged actions
- Separation of duties (e.g., code author not solely approving deployment in sensitive systems)
