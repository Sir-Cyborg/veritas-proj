3. Principles

CBG follows these secure development principles:

- Security by design: threat modeling and architectural risk controls are addressed early, not after deployment.

- Least privilege and secure defaults: services run with minimal permissions; insecure configurations are prohibited.

- Continuous verification: automated security checks are embedded in pipelines and required for release.

- Dependency hygiene: third-party libraries are managed and updated based on risk.

- Accountability: changes are attributable; approvals and evidence are retained.
