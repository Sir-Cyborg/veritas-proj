5.2 Architecture and Design

Threat modeling is required for new applications and significant changes to critical
services. Architecture must include:

- Identity and access patterns (MFA, service-to-service authentication, token scopes).

- Data protection mechanisms (encryption, tokenization where applicable).

- Logging and monitoring requirements for fraud/security detection.

- Resilience patterns (rate limiting, circuit breakers, failover, idempotency for transactional flows).
