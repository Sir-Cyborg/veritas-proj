1. Introduction

Operational resilience is essential for Corporate Banking Group (CBG). Backup and Disaster
Recovery (DR) capabilities enable the restoration of data and services after accidental
deletion, corruption, ransomware events, infrastructure failures, or site-level outages.

This procedure defines minimum requirements and steps for backup creation, validation,
retention, recovery execution, and disaster recovery testing for ICT services. It ensures
that recovery objectives are defined, achievable, and evidenced.
