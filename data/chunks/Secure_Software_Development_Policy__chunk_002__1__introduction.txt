1. Introduction

Software delivered by Corporate Banking Group (CBG) enables critical customer and internal
processes, including payments, lending, onboarding, and reporting. Security defects in
software can lead to fraud, data breaches, regulatory breaches, and operational disruption.

This Secure Software Development Policy defines mandatory security requirements to be
integrated throughout the software development lifecycle (SDLC), from planning and design to
implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance. It applies to in-house developed
software and to externally sourced software where CBG controls configuration or code.
