4. Roles and Responsibilities

- Change Requestor: prepares the change record with required detail and coordinates implementation.

- Implementer: executes the change steps and validates outcomes.

- Service Owner: accountable for business impact decisions and approves changes for their service.

- CAB: reviews and approves normal changes based on risk/impact; ensures scheduling coordination.

- Change Manager: governs the process, ensures record quality, and chairs CAB meetings.

- Information Security/IAM: reviews changes that affect security controls or sensitive environments.
