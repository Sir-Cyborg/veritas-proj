6. Risk Assessment and Approval

Approval levels depend on risk and scope. Factors include:

- Customer impact potential, including digital channel availability and payment processing.

- Data integrity risk (transaction postings, balances).

- Security impact (IAM policies, encryption, firewall rules).

- Complexity and novelty of the change.

Low-risk standard changes may be auto-approved but must follow the documented procedure.
Normal changes require CAB or delegated approval. High-risk changes require additional
approvals, including Information Security review where applicable.
