4. Third-Party Classification and Criticality

Third parties must be classified based on:

- Data sensitivity: whether Confidential or Restricted data is processed.

- Service criticality: whether the service supports critical banking functions (payments, authentication, customer channels).

- Connectivity: network integration depth and remote access requirements.

- Replaceability and concentration: ease of switching, market availability, dependency level.

Critical third parties require enhanced due diligence, resilience assessment, and executive
oversight.
