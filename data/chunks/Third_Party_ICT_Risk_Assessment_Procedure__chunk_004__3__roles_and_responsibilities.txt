3. Roles and Responsibilities

- Business Owner: sponsors the engagement, defines service requirements, and owns business impact decisions.

- ICT Service Owner: validates technical integration, architecture, and operational controls.

- TPRM (ICT): coordinates due diligence, risk assessments, and ongoing monitoring; maintains third-party inventory.

- Information Security: performs security assessment and defines security requirements; reviews high-risk findings.

- Procurement and Legal: ensures contracts include required clauses; manages negotiation and supplier obligations.

- Continuity/Resilience Team: assesses resilience, exit planning, and recovery capabilities for critical services.
