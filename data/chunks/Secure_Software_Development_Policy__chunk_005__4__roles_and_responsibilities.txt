4. Roles and Responsibilities

- Product/Service Owner: accountable for business risk decisions and prioritization of remediation work.

- Engineering Manager: ensures the team follows this policy and maintains secure delivery practices.

- Developers: implement secure coding practices, fix findings, and maintain dependency health.

- Security Champions: act as embedded security contacts within teams; assist with threat modeling and triage.

- Application Security (AppSec): provides standards, tools, training, and performs assurance activities.

- Platform/DevOps Team: ensures CI/CD and runtime platforms enforce baseline controls.
