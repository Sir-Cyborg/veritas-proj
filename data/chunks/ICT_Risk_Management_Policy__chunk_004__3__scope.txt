3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All ICT services, applications, infrastructure components, and supporting processes used by CBG.

- All change programs and projects introducing new technology or materially changing existing services.

- All third-party ICT services and outsourcing arrangements, including cloud and managed services.

The policy covers risks related to information security, availability, continuity, change
and release, data management, technology obsolescence, and supplier dependency.
