7. Key ICT Risk Domains and Minimum Controls

CBG maintains a risk taxonomy that includes (at minimum) the following domains and expected
controls:

- Cybersecurity risk: identity controls, hardening, vulnerability management, monitoring, incident response.

- Resilience and continuity risk: backup/recovery, disaster recovery testing, capacity management, high availability architecture.

- Change and release risk: change governance, segregation of duties, testing, rollback plans, emergency change control.

- Data risk: classification, retention, encryption, data loss prevention, integrity controls for transactional data.

- Obsolescence risk: lifecycle management, upgrade planning, vendor supportability tracking.

- Third-party risk: due diligence, contract controls, exit planning, concentration management.
