2. Scope

This procedure applies to changes affecting:

- Production systems and services.

- Shared platforms (identity, network, databases, monitoring).

- Security controls and configurations (firewall rules, IAM policies).

- Cloud infrastructure and infrastructure-as-code deployments.

Routine operational activities that do not change state (e.g., log review) are not changes.
Patching, configuration adjustments, and deployments are changes and must follow this
procedure.
