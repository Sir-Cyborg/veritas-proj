3. Roles and Responsibilities

- Data Owner: accountable for classification, access rules, and retention decisions for a dataset or information domain.

- Data Custodian (often ICT): implements technical controls in systems storing/processing the data.

- Users: handle data according to classification and report misclassification or mishandling.

- Information Security: defines protective control expectations and monitors compliance.

- Compliance/Legal: interprets regulatory and contractual requirements.
