5. Change Record Requirements

All changes must be recorded in the ITSM tool and include at minimum:

- Scope and affected services/configuration items.

- Business justification and expected benefits.

- Risk assessment (impact, likelihood, rollback complexity).

- Implementation plan with step-by-step tasks.

- Testing evidence and validation plan.

- Rollback/backout plan and decision criteria for aborting.

- Communication plan and stakeholder notifications.

- Scheduling details and maintenance window alignment.
